## Five files, one tool in each

The course has five lessons, and each one opens an old file that teaches you one tool:

- **File 1, thalidomide:** how to turn a hunch into a claim that a study can test.
- **File 2, DES, a hormone drug, and a rare cancer:** how to choose a fair group to compare the cases with.
- **File 3, smoking and lung cancer:** how to read a two-by-two table and its odds ratio.
- **File 4, the contraceptive pill and blood clots:** how the way a study is run can build a large ratio by itself, which is called bias.
- **File 5, doctors and miners followed for years:** how a cohort study follows people forward and measures the risk itself.

## Each tool goes straight to your case

After each old file, you use its tool on the plastics plant case. By the end of File 5 you can close that case with an answer that the numbers support.

**Every lesson works the same way: you learn a tool on a real file, then you use it on your own case.**

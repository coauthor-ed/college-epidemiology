## Six steps in every file

Every file, old or live, is worked through the same six steps:

1. **Claim:** say exactly who, what exposure, what disease, how much and how long.
2. **Study design:** choose a kind of study that can test the claim.
3. **Table:** sort the people into a two-by-two table of counts.
4. **Ratio:** work out one number for how strongly the exposure and the disease go together.
5. **Bias:** check whether the way the study was run could have made that number.
6. **Verdict:** give your answer on the claim, and say what the study cannot tell you.

## Real files and an invented case

The old files are real studies, and their numbers are the ones the investigators published. The plastics plant case is invented for practice: the plant, the city and every number in it are made up, and each slide that shows its numbers says so. It is modelled on a real finding, which you will meet at the end of File 5.

**The old files teach the method, and your own case is where you use it.**

## You join an investigation unit

You are a new investigator in a unit that studies the causes of disease, a field called epidemiology. Your work is to find out whether something people do, take or breathe causes an illness, and to show it with numbers that other people can check.

<!-- STEP -->
## The senior investigator

A senior investigator in the unit is your mentor. You will not see this person on screen; their advice reaches you as short notes on the case files, marked "Senior's note". They teach you in two ways: they hand you old files that are already closed, so that you can learn from how real investigators worked, and they give you one live case of your own.

<!-- STEP -->
## Your live case: a plastics plant

A hospital in an Indian city has seen a number of men with a rare liver tumour. All of them worked at the same plastics plant, and many of them cleaned the large tanks where the plastic is made. Over the five files, your job is to find out whether cleaning the tanks could cause this tumour.

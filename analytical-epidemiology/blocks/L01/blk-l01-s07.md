## The live case opens

*Illustrative case: the plant, the city and every number are invented for this course.*

A hospital in an Indian city has diagnosed 20 men with a rare liver tumour over a few years. All 20 worked at the same plastics plant, and several of them cleaned the large tanks where the plastic is made. The hospital has asked for an investigation.

> Senior's note: Write the claim you would test before you collect anything.

<!-- STEP -->
## Choose each part

For each of the five parts, the board offers two wordings. Pick one for each part, and watch the list of data you would need grow under the claim. Choose the wording that a record at the plant or the hospital could actually confirm.

<!-- STEP -->
## What if you choose the vague wording?

Try "exposure to chemicals" as the cause, or "eventually" as the time. That part of the list now reads "cannot be measured", because no record can tell "eventually" from "never". A precise claim reads: among men who worked at the plant, at least 5 years of tank cleaning causes this tumour, with more years giving more risk, appearing 10 or more years after first exposure.

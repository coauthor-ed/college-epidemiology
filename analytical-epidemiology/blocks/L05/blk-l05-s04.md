## A Boston file from the same drawer

In 1978 investigators opened the records of one Boston hospital and pulled the files of 17,080 babies born there between 1969 and 1975. They separated the labours that had used electronic foetal monitoring from the labours that had not, and compared neonatal deaths, deaths of newborn babies, in the two groups. Every birth and every death in the study happened years before the investigators began.

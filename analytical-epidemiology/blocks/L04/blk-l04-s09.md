## The stamp no check can remove

The odds ratio now carries a second marking: no incidence. The 13.2 says how strongly reactor cleaning and the tumour travel together in this sample. It cannot say how many of all the plant's reactor workers fall ill each year, because no group was followed forward. The senior's note supplies the missing thing: twenty years of records, with the job and department of every man who entered the plant. **What if you start from the workers rather than from the patients?**

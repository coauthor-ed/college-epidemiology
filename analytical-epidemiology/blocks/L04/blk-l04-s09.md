## The stamp no check can remove

Even with every check ticked, the odds ratio carries a second marking: no incidence. The 13.2 says how strongly tank cleaning and the tumour travel together among the 80 men studied. It cannot say how many of all the plant's tank cleaners fall ill each year, because no group was ever followed forward.

## What the design is good at, from the files themselves

The method chapter lists the case-control study's strengths and limits (Table 13), and every line belongs to a file you have already opened. All seven strengths belong to the DES file. The disease was so rare that a cohort would have waited years for its first cases. Eight cases and 32 controls gave an answer within months and at low cost. Several possible causes were examined at once, nobody had to be followed or could be lost, and nothing was done to the women, so the ethical problems were few.

## The limits, in the same files

The six limits sort across the other files. The recall question shows one: memory and old records cannot be checked. The hospital door shows another: a suitable comparison group is hard to choose.

Your own odds ratio shows the other four. It gives no incidence. It cannot separate the cause from the factors that travel with it. It is not the design for testing a treatment, and its cases and controls may not represent their populations.

## What the design is good at, from the files themselves

The method chapter on the desk lists the case-control study's strengths and limits in two numbered columns (Table 13). Each line belongs to an earlier file that showed it. Seven strengths belong to the DES file, because cold case 2 needed exactly this design:

- a disease so rare that a cohort would wait years for cases;
- a handful of subjects;
- speed;
- low cost;
- several possible causes examined at once;
- no follow-up years to lose anyone through;
- almost nothing done to the participants, so few ethical problems.

Eight cases, an answer within months, and nothing done to anyone.

<!-- STEP -->
## The limits, in the same files

The six limits sort across the other three files. One belongs to the recall question: information from memory or old records, whose accuracy cannot be checked. One belongs to the hospital door: the difficulty of choosing a suitable comparison group. Four belong to your own odds ratio:

- it cannot measure incidence;
- it cannot separate the true cause from the factors that travel with it;
- it is not the design for evaluating a treatment;
- whether its cases and controls truly represent their populations stays uncertain.

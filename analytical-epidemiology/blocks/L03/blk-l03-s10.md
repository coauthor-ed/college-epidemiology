## What the number says, and what it does not

An odds ratio this large is a strong association, and if the disease is rare, as the last exercise showed, it is also a fair estimate of the relative risk. It does not yet say how the association came about. A number can only be as good as the way the people behind it were chosen and asked.

## The senior's note

> A ratio this large can be built by the way the data were collected.

That is not a reason to doubt the count; it is a question about the design. The patients were interviewed on the ward and the controls were asked at home, and the two groups were chosen in different ways. **Before the number can be believed, the way the study was run has to be checked.** What in the way you ran the study could have made it?

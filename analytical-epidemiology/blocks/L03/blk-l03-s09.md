## The live case's table, as the records give it

*Illustrative numbers: the plant, the city and every count are invented for this course.*

Twenty men with the tumour and 60 controls from the same plant have been interviewed. Fourteen of the 20 had cleaned the reactors, against 9 of the 60 controls. The dots sort into the cells: 14, 9, 6 and 51, with margins of 20 and 60.

## Fill the cross-product before the scene shows it

The odds ratio multiplies the two diagonal cells and divides one product by the other. Work out 14 × 51 and 9 × 6 on paper first, then let the scene take them. The larger product belongs on top.

<!-- STEP -->
## 714 over 54

Fourteen times 51 is 714, and 9 times 6 is 54, so the odds ratio is 714 / 54 = 13.2. **The odds of reactor work were 13.2 times as high among the men with the tumour.** Whether that number can be trusted is the question this file closes on.
